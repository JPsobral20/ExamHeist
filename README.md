## Description

- 2D platformer game developed in Unity. Player must avoid being detected by guards and cameras and steal the exam from the professor's office.
- Hide in closets to prevent being detected by guards and cameras and not activate the alarm system.
- Collect keys to unlock locked doors, collect the pen and papers to access the professors computer and reveal it's password.
- Exit the building to successfully complete the heist!

**Tools:** Unity, C#
